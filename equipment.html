Equipment & Capabilities

Modern, efficient equipment for high and low volume parts. Our high-speed presses run compound and progressive dies efficiently.

Charmaran's modern, efficient equipment allows us to run high and low volume parts. Our high-speed presses run compound and progressive dies efficiently, allowing us to process both large and small orders. All presses are equipped with coil cradles and straighteners up to .170 thicknesses, and Rapid Air Feeds are used on all progressive dies for accurate feed progressions.
