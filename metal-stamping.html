Metal Stamping Services

Short, medium, or long runs — Charmaran is your one source for washers, shims, and precision stampings.

Stamping · Drawing · Welding · Fabrication · Tooling · Design

Many Charmaran customers require J.I.T. shipments and stocking programs to reduce their internal costs, and our partnership agreements meet these needs. We carry the inventory, manufacture the parts, and release as scheduled to annual blanket orders.

No run is too small or too large. We process orders from as few as 50 pieces to runs over a million pieces. Whatever the size of your order, you can depend on Charmaran to produce custom stampings quickly, efficiently, and competitively.
